Your calls. Your files. Your AI.

Simstim records calls on your Mac, transcribes them on the same Mac and saves each one as a Markdown file you own. What you promised and what others promised you go into one list beside your notes. Audio never leaves your computer.

How it works

  1. Record

    Press Record. Simstim captures your microphone and the other side of the call as two separate channels. It can suggest recording when it notices a call, but it never starts on its own. Let the other people know before you record.

  2. Transcribe on your Mac

    A live transcript appears while you talk. After you stop, a second pass with a larger model improves accuracy. Both models run locally. You can take notes during the call, and they stay in their own section.

  3. Keep plain files

    Each call becomes a Markdown file with the recording beside it, in a folder you choose. Search across past calls, click a timestamp to hear that moment, or continue a recording into the same note later. With an assistant connected, a summary follows, ending with the call's open loops.

You will not forget to press Record

When Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, Telegram or a browser tab starts using the microphone, a small banner appears in the corner of your screen. One click starts recording. Simstim never starts on its own: noticing a call is not permission to record it.

  • Two dots while you talk. "you" and "them" light up while each side is being heard, so a muted microphone or a missing permission shows in the first minute, not after the call.
  • A timer in the menu bar. Simstim sits in the menu bar too: the elapsed time while recording, Stop and Add note without switching windows.
  • Knows when the call ends. The banner comes back with Stop, or Simstim stops by itself if you turn that on.

Every promise from every call, in one file

A call ends with things you agreed to do, things others agreed to do for you and questions nobody answered. Simstim keeps them in Open loops.md beside your notes, in four sections: Mine, Waiting for, Open questions and Done.

After each summary, Simstim adds the call's new loops to the file and checks off the ones the call reports as done. Every item links to the second in the recording where it was agreed, so the exact wording is one click away.

Check an item off in Simstim or in any other editor. Done keeps four weeks of history, then clears itself. Everything else is yours: Simstim adds lines and checks lines, and never rewords or deletes what you wrote.

  • Waiting for is the part no to-do app can write. What other people owe you exists only in the conversation, and Simstim was listening.
  • Not a task manager. No priorities, reminders or badges. One Markdown file with GitHub-style checkboxes, readable in Obsidian, git and every editor.
  • Needs a summary. Open loops are written by the same assistant as your summaries, so they pause when automatic summaries are off. One switch in Settings.

Files are the database

There is no export step because there is nothing to export from. Open the folder in Obsidian, keep it in git, sync it with the service you already use. Every other tool on your Mac can read these files today and in twenty years.

Your notes, the summary and the transcript live in separate sections. Re-transcribing a call replaces only the transcript, never what you wrote.

Optional AI. Yours, not ours.

Connect Claude or ChatGPT to get a summary after each call, keep your open loops current and ask questions across your notes. Simstim installs the official helper and opens the provider's own sign-in. If you prefer, use your own OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter API key, stored in macOS Keychain.

Simstim sells no credits and adds no fee. Your plan, your limits, your bill.

  • Text only. Summaries, open loops and questions send the note's text to your provider, never audio.
  • Off means off. Without AI, recording, transcription, notes, playback and search keep working.
  • Nothing to rebuild. For a PDF, a deck or a follow-up email, hand the file to your assistant. It already has those tools.

Plain answers

Privacy

  • No Simstim account, no hosted notes, no analytics.
  • Audio is recorded and transcribed on your Mac and stays there.
  • Network use is model downloads, a daily check for a new version, the optional AI setup and the AI requests you make.
  • Deleting a note moves it to the Trash. Removing the app leaves your notes alone.

Limitations

  • Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 14.2 or later today. Windows and Linux versions are coming soon.
  • "Me" and "Them" are your microphone and system audio, not individual speakers.
  • Names and numbers can be misheard. Check important details against the recording.
  • Open loops come from summaries, so they need a connected assistant and start with your next summarized call.

The details are in the privacy document.
